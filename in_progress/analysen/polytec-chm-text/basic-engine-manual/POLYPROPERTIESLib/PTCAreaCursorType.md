*Quelle: `POLYPROPERTIESLib~PTCAreaCursorType.html` · Polytec PolyProperties Type Library*

|---|---|---|
| ptcAreaCursorTypeHorizontal | 1 | Horizontal cursors of a spectrogram or Campbell diagram. |
| ptcAreaCursorTypeNone | 0 | No cursors. |
| ptcAreaCursorTypeOrder | 3 | Order cursors of a Campbell diagram. |
| ptcAreaCursorTypeVertical | 2 | Vertical cursors of a spectrogram or Campbell diagram. |
